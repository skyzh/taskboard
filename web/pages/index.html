Each review improves the next

Tickets

Checkpoint what agents found.

Agents record what they learn and decide as they work, with evidence and open questions. When they pause, the ticket’s next step lets another session pick up the work.

Knowledge

Carry lessons into the next session.

When agents learn something reusable, they save it in Knowledge with a link to the evidence. Later sessions build on those lessons.

Inbox

Answer when you have time.

When agents need your decision, they ask in your Inbox and carry on with work that can proceed independently. Your answer stays with the work and informs the next review.
